# PanzaScope Code of Conduct

## My Pledge

As the creator and maintainer of PanzaScope, I am committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

I pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy development environment.

## Standards of Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for this project include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
* Being respectful of differing opinions, architectural viewpoints, and development experiences.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
* Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using or improving it.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative technical setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

As the project maintainer, I am responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. I will take appropriate, fair, and corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

I reserve the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and I will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all repository spaces (including Issues and Discussions), and also applies when an individual is publicly interacting regarding PanzaScope in external spaces (such as Reddit or Discord threads).

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to me at:
**panzadabira@gmail.com**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly, fairly, and with absolute confidentiality. I am obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

I will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences for any action deemed in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction
* **Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unwelcome.
* **Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
* **Impact**: A violation through a single major incident or a series of actions.
* **Consequence**: A formal warning. Continued behavior or unsolicited interaction with those involved (including myself during enforcement) will lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban
* **Impact**: A serious violation of project standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.
* **Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication within the PanzaScope repository spaces for a specified period of time.

### 4. Permanent Ban
* **Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of project standards, or a single egregious violation (such as targeted harassment or leaking private info).
* **Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the PanzaScope project.
